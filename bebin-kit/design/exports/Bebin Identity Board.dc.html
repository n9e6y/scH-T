Identity & design system board, v1
An in-person shopping assistant for Tehran, by torob. Two hues, the radish and its leaves. Saturation encodes how sure we are.
Palette
Two OKLCH hue families: red h 0–12, green h 103–166. Neutrals are the green family at chroma below 0.02. Chroma rises with certainty.
Type
Vazirmatn for every functional string. Lalezar for the wordmark, the home hero line and empty-state headlines only. Latin wordmarks are drawn, not typeset.
Mascot
One 24×24 sprite with a beet outline. Eight poses are small overlays on that base. Integer scales only, one per screen, never in lists.
Layout
Six 390px columns: identity, mascot, foundations, controls and shell, trust components, composition and colour-blind check.
Self-check
No uniform card grid: receipts, stamps and dashed unknowns carry the look. Hue audit: every value on this board resolves to red or green.
Logo set
One lens geometry (r 21, stroke 14) shared by look and torob.

Red splits by lightness, not chroma: radish sits at L .56–.75 and is filled; beet sits at L .36 and is only ever ink on its tint. Stamp ink (.186) is the single most saturated value in the system, just above radish (.180). Text-safe green tops out near .14, so verified text uses #007A36 and the graphic ink carries the peak.
